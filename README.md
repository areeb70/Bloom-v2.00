# Bloom 
Welcome to Bloom 🌸

Imagine a kind, patient coach who lives in your pocket. Bloom doesn't throw you into the deep end of a party and tell you to "just be confident." Instead, it breaks social confidence down into tiny, manageable micro-steps—what psychologists call Graded Exposure.
It’s a game: You start as a "Seedling" doing things like making eye contact or saying "hi" to a neighbor.
You level up: As you complete tasks, you earn points, maintain a streak, and grow into a Sprout, Leaf, Stem, and finally Bloom.
It’s private: Your journey (thoughts, anxiety scores, history) stays 100% on your device. No cloud, no accounts required (Guest mode works fully).
It’s reflective: After every task, you pause. How anxious was I? What actually happened? This rewires your brain to realize: "Oh, that wasn't catastrophic. I handled it."

The Problem: Social anxiety isn't a character flaw; it’s a prediction error. Your brain predicts "Danger!" for safe situations (ordering coffee, saying hello). Avoidance reinforces the error.

The Bloom Fix: You don't "fix" yourself. You collect data.

Predict: "If I say hi, they'll ignore me / I'll freeze." (Anxiety: 8/10)
Test: You do the micro-task (Say "Hi").
Reflect: "They smiled back. I felt awkward for 3 seconds, then it passed." (Reality: Safe).
Update: Your brain logs: Prediction wrong. Situation safe.
Bloom automates this loop. The Reflection Screen is the engine. The Streak is the consistency. The Levels are the curriculum. You aren't "doing therapy"—you're just playing a game where the side effect is freedom.

### Getting Started

How it Works: 🌿 Grow Through Levels: Start as a Seedling with low-tension tasks (like a simple smile) and gradually evolve into a Sprout, Leaf, Stem, and finally, Bloom, as you tackle more challenging social goals.

Open the app → Tap "Continue as Guest".
Optional: Sign in with Google/Email later if you want cloud backup.

Choose a theme color (Teal, Purple, Orange, etc.). This colors your garden. Makes it feel like yours.

Tap a level card: Seedling → Sprout → Leaf → Stem → Bloom.
Start with Seedling. Tasks are microscopic (Eye contact, Holding a door, Saying "Thanks").
Tap "Tap to start challenge".

Read the task. Example: "Make eye contact and smile at one person today."
Go do it in real life. Put the phone in your pocket.
Come back. Tap "I Completed This".

Slider: "How anxious did you feel? (1–10)" → Be honest.
Text Box: "What actually happened?" → Write 1 sentence. "Cashier smiled. Felt nervous for 2 sec then fine."
Tap "Finish Reflection".
Points added. Streak updated. Task logged in History.

Open app. See your Streak: 1 Day.
Tap Map → New random Seedling task.
Pro Tip: After 10 tasks in Seedling, a popup suggests moving to Sprout. You decide when you're ready.

🛠 Pro Tips for the First Week:
Buy a "Streak Freeze" (Shop → 50 pts) immediately once you have points. Life happens; protect your momentum.
Check History (Map → "View Growth Journey"). Reading past wins on bad days is powerful.
Language Switch (Profile → Language) if you think in another language—reflection works best in your native tongue.
Reset is Safe (Profile → Reset All Progress). If you want a clean slate, it wipes only local data. No penalty.


Tap "Continue as Guest." or login with email or google login. Do one tiny thing. Reflect. Watch the garden grow.
